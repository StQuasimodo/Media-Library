https://github.com/StQuasimodo/Media-Library/tree/main/android-tv
LibVLC remains in shared native libraries. No LibVLC source was modified.
Users may rebuild My Cinema with a compatible replacement of these libraries.
See docs/TV-PLAYBACK.md for dependency source and relinking instructions.

Dual subtitle playback uses the JNI and native libraries from mpv-android release
2024-11-16 (ARMv7 and x86). The original archives and their matching build
scripts and dependency definitions are available at:
https://github.com/mpv-android/mpv-android/releases/tag/2024-11-16
